 Type in your take-home pay and see it split into needs, wants and savings. It starts at 50/30/20, but you set your own percentages with sliders and build your own categories inside each bucket, as fixed amounts or shares. Log each paycheck and a History tab keeps a running total of what you set aside, month by month, with room to add past months by hand. You can also pull a paycheck from a bank CSV export. Your data stays on your device, and it installs on your iPhone home screen like an app.
